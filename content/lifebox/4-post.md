# Stage 4. The post

**4.1 Post.** Two metre pressure-treated class 4 timber, driven **800 mm** in, box at **500 mm**. A half square metre panel is about 170 N at 25 m/s and 155 Nm of overturning moment, which is why the post is not shorter and why the panel brackets are **bolted through** with M8 (security heads) rather than hose clamps, which loosen as timber shrinks.

**4.2 Probes, 1.5 m south of the post**, on their own 2 m leads. South because the panel's shadow always falls north. Dig wider than 20 cm, insert the prongs **horizontally into the undisturbed side wall** at 10 cm and 30 cm, never hammer, backfill in layer order and firm to natural density. Air gaps read low for months. Wait 24 to 48 hours before trusting a number.

**4.3 Register the real place** and its three devices, swap the tokens in `life-node.env`, watch the first upload arrive. Note the probe position to a metre and mark it, so that machinery misses it and a person finds it in five years. Arm the guard; hand the landowner the maintenance rule: a green LED means safe to open.

## Gate

`lastSeenAt` moves within one interval on all three devices, and the next morning's dawn chorus shows up as detections.
