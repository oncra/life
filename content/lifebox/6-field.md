## Make the field software

In the field there is no WiFi, and the box should listen ten hours a day in summer (eight from dawn, two from sunset) instead of fifteen minutes an hour. Both are set by the software.

1. On [your box page](https://life.oncra.org/box), choose **Another WiFi? Make new software for this box**, leave both WiFi fields **empty**, and press the button.
2. Download it and put it on the memory card as before.

The box keeps its name and its history. The old card stops sending.

## Set the post

![The finished box on a wooden post at a field edge, panel above the green box, two probe cables running to a small soil pit](../../public/img/build/s4-post.webp "Rendered impression. Post 80 cm deep, box at 50 cm, probes 1.5 m to the south.")

1. Choose a spot at the edge of a field or path, where machines do not drive. Mark it on the farmer's map.
2. Drive the **2 m post** 80 cm into the ground.
3. Fix the box on the post at knee height (about 50 cm), on the side that gets the least sun.
4. Bolt the panel brackets **through** the post with the M8 bolts, panel facing south, above the box so it shades it. A panel is a sail in a storm: straps loosen, bolts do not.

## Put the probes in the soil

1. Dig a small pit, a bit wider than a spade, **1.5 m south** of the post, where the panel's shadow never falls.
2. Push the **10 cm** probe sideways into the untouched wall of the pit, at 10 cm deep. The **30 cm** probe (the taped one) the same way at 30 cm. Push, never hammer.
3. Fill the pit with the soil in the order it came out, and press it firm. Air around the prongs reads dry for months.
4. Lead both cables to the box, through their glands.

## Switch it on and tell the box where it is

1. Put the card with the field software in, connect the battery, and tap the Witty Pi's button.
2. Standing next to the box with your phone, open [your box page](https://life.oncra.org/box) and press **The box is here**. That puts it on the map and starts the satellite reading of the place around it. Your exact location stays hidden: the public sees a circle of about a kilometre.

> **Check.** Within the hour, every light on your box page shows a fresh time. The morning after, **Microphone** shows the birds of the dawn chorus.
