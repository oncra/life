title: "Build a Life Node"
short: "From ordering the parts to a box on a post that reports by itself, one step at a time."
---

A Life Node listens for birds, reads two soil probes and sends what it hears and feels to the [Life Oracle](https://life.oncra.org), on solar power and 4G. You need no programming and no command line. You need a laptop, a free afternoon for the desk part, a few evenings for the box, and a spade.

**How this page works.** All the steps are on this one page, in order. The column on the left shows where you are; tick a step when it is done and the page remembers it on this device. Every step on the desk ends with a check you can see on [your box page](https://life.oncra.org/box): a light that turns green when the box has sent what that step was about. Go to the next step when the light is green, not before.
