## Order the parts

The list below is everything for one box, grouped by shop so the parts arrive in as few parcels as possible. Each line links to the product. Prices were checked on the date in the list and include VAT. A few things come from a DIY shop: they are at the bottom.

You can start the desk steps as soon as the **Raspberry Pi, the Witty Pi, the memory card, the microphone, the soil probes, the RS485 adapter and the 4G stick** are in. The battery, the panel, the box and the post can follow later.

Two choices to make before you order:

- **The solar panel.** Where the box stands in the open (grass, a field edge): 40 to 50 W is enough. Where a crop will grow over it: 100 W.
- **The SIM.** The 1NCE IoT card is ordered online and arrives by post. It lasts ten years without a monthly bill.
