## Paint the box

The box is plastic, and sunlight makes plastic brittle in a few years. Paint it **matt green**, outside, while it is still empty: one coat of plastic primer, then two thin coats of outdoor paint for plastic. The paint is its sun cream first and its camouflage second. Let it dry for a day.

## Lay everything out on plastic

![The painted green enclosure open on the bench, boards and battery laid out on a white plastic plate, glands in the bottom wall](../../public/img/build/s3-box.webp "Rendered impression. Battery flat on the floor, glands pointing down, nothing metal near the 4G stick.")

1. Leave the **steel plate** that came with the box out. Steel next to the 4G stick weakens its signal.
2. Cut the **3 mm plastic sheet** to fit the inside of the box.
3. Lay the battery flat on the floor of the box first: it takes half the space and decides where the rest goes.
4. Mount the Pi with the Witty Pi, the RS485 adapter and the controller on the plastic sheet with the nylon standoffs. Keep the 4G stick as far from the battery as you can.

## Make the hole the microphone listens through

1. Drill a **5 mm hole** in the side wall, at the height where the microphone will sit.
2. Stick the waterproof **membrane** over the hole on the inside.
3. Press the microphone board flat against the wall over the hole, with its small sound hole facing the wall, and fix it with a ring of foam tape. Any gap between board and wall whistles.
4. On the outside, glue the small **cap with foam** inside it over the hole, open side down, so rain and wind stay off.

## Five cable glands, all pointing down

Drill five holes in the **bottom** wall for the glands: one for the panel cable, two for the probe cables, one **vented** gland at the lowest point so the box can breathe, and one spare that stays closed. Tighten each gland around its cable until the cable cannot be pulled.

## Spray the boards

Cover the microphone's sound hole and every plug and socket with tape. Then spray the boards with a thin layer of the clear **Plastik 70** lacquer, outside, and let it dry for an hour. It keeps damp air off the electronics for years. Take the tape off.

## Close it and hose it

1. Close the lid and let the box run for an hour.
2. Then spray it with a garden hose for five minutes, from every side.
3. Open it and look for water. Any drop: find where it came in and seal that gland or seam again.

![The closed green enclosure being sprayed with a garden hose](../../public/img/build/s3-hose.webp "Rendered impression. Five minutes from every side, then open and look.")

> **Check.** Dry inside after the hose, and on your box page **4G** is still green with the lid closed. The 4G stick works through the plastic.
