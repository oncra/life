Life Clips

Takes the sound clips off a Life Node at a visit, and sends them to the Life Oracle.

Open Life Clips from the address it printed in its window: this address lacks its key.

1Connect to the node

The WiFi name and password are on the node's page in the Life Oracle. The node's WiFi is on only during a visit.

Not connected to a node.

2Download the clips

Each clip is checked against its fingerprint (SHA-256), then the node signs the checked clips over to this laptop. The node keeps its own copies until the oracle has them.

3Send to the oracle

Works whenever this laptop is online, also days after the visit.
